# Dashboard UI audit — 2026-10-02

1. **HIGH — Focus indicators are not reliably visible in dark mode** (R-32, R-34).
   The global focus style uses a translucent teal tint on the dark dashboard surface. Its contrast is too low to consistently show keyboard focus. Replace it with an opaque, high-contrast focus ring.

2. **HIGH — Toolbar icon controls are below the mobile touch-target size** (R-03).
   The sidebar and theme controls are 38 by 38 pixels. Increase their interactive hit area to at least 44 by 44 pixels without changing the visual hierarchy.
